Privacy Policy

CardFront Conquest respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, CardFront Conquest may collect:

  • Account information, such as commander username, recovery email, and authentication identifiers.
  • Gameplay information, such as card inventory, resources, commander progress, game state, lobbies, alliances, marketplace activity, and battle notifications.
  • Community content you choose to submit, such as forum posts, replies, alliance messages, and direct messages.
  • Purchase validation information for optional App Store purchases, including transaction identifiers and signed transaction data.
  • Push notification tokens and notification preferences if you allow notifications.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Sync progress and restore gameplay across sessions and devices.
  • Operate multiplayer, marketplace, alliance, community, and notification features.
  • Validate App Store purchases and deliver purchased or earned in-app items.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce game integrity, and maintain app security.
  • Respond to support requests.

Third-Party Services

CardFront Conquest uses Supabase to provide backend services such as authentication, database storage, and server-side game operations. The app also uses Apple services for App Store purchases and push notifications. These services process data according to their own privacy and security practices.

Advertising and Tracking

CardFront Conquest does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell personal information. The app does not use data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, or to protect the app, users, or the public.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to create an account, provide a recovery email, participate in community features, make purchases, or allow push notifications. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain game records, resolve disputes, enforce rules, comply with legal obligations, and support account recovery.

Children's Privacy

CardFront Conquest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cardfrontconquest@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
